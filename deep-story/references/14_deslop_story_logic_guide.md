# Story Validity and Change (故事成立与变化): Diagnosing Causality, Repairing by Authorization

This file examines the relationships among events, actions, information, and outcomes. For sentence- and paragraph-level expression, see [expression.md](12_deslop_expression_guide.md); for the level of detail (详略) of existing content, see [narrative-focus.md](13_deslop_narrative_focus_guide.md). Execution scope is determined by the caller; review-only mode (审稿) only reports.

## Inspect Along the Events, Without Forcing a Conflict Formula

First confirm from the original text and existing design the characters' circumstances, known information, this scene's function, and the outcome, then press further on how the outcome occurs. Characters may be passive, impulsive, mistaken, or yielding/forbearing (忍让); everyday, unhurried, and atmospheric scenes also have value. Do not require every scene to have an opponent, a reversal, a cost, or a win/loss.

| Inspection Target (检查对象) | Actionable Questions (可执行的问题) | Gaps to Pinpoint (需要定位的缺口) |
|---|---|---|
| Basis for action (行为依据) | Given what they knew at that moment, why would they choose this step? Do fear, habit, or misjudgment have textual support? | Suddenly trusting, rebelling, or forgiving solely because the author needs it; a character temporarily changing personality for the sake of the outcome |
| Causal linkage (因果承接) | Which condition in the antecedent triggers the consequent? Were the intermediate conditions already written, or do they not exist at all? | "Then" (“然后”) stringing together a chain of events; explanatory sentences claiming success without a process that makes it hold up (成立过程) |
| Others' stances (他人立场) | What is the other party trying to protect, and what constraints do they have? Why do they accept or change their strategy? | Everyone else is only responsible for helping, delivering clues, being astonished, or admitting fault, with no reasons of their own |
| Method and outcome (办法与结果) | Are the solution, mistake, and gain supported by prior text? Is the weight of the outcome commensurate with the preparation? | Evidence or ability appearing out of nowhere to solve the problem; a single request bypassing already-established resistance |
| Change and carry-over (变化与承接) | After the experience, what changes in relationships, understanding, resources, expectations, emotions, or room for action? Does the subsequent text pick it up? | Consecutive scenes resetting to the original state; the same method working repeatedly; events that already happened having no effect |
| Information and chronology (信息与时序) | Who knows what, when, and from what source? When does the reader learn it? | Characters knowing answers ahead of time, treating recollection as on-scene presence, or spoiling causality not yet revealed |

Record items one by one only when there is a problem; normal items can be skipped—do not backtrack to add scenes just to fill out the checklist. Judging something as "too flat" (“过平”) requires pointing out repetition or absence in the original text: for example, repeated requests for help being rejected without ever adjusting the method, or a designed shift in relationship failing to affect later behavior. A single instance of caregiving without conflict cannot be judged flat on that basis.

## Is What's Missing the Writing Technique, or the Enabling Conditions?

When encountering an abrupt shift, first search the preceding text, worldbuilding/settings (设定), and outline provided in this session. There are three possible conclusions:

- **The basis already exists, but the main text failed to connect it (依据已有，正文没接上)**: Restore the linkage within the established information boundaries, avoiding repetitive explanation. If an established promise only appeared in earlier text, the character can be allowed to use it in the current negotiation.
- **Insufficient material; cannot judge for now (资料不足，暂时不能判断)**: Specify clearly which item is unknown and continue evidence-backed revision; do not judge the entire book as lacking setup just because the full book was not provided, and do not invent prior events on the author's behalf.
- **The current design truly lacks conditions (当前设计确实缺条件)**: Explain which choice or outcome in the original text lacks what support, and give a minimal design option along with its impact. Adding explanations like "he was finally moved" (“他终于被打动”) cannot supply the other party's stance.

Distinguish information gaps from suspense (信息缺口 vs. 悬念): wet footprints left outside the door while it is not yet known who came form a clear, specific question; you should not guess the answer for the author. Only when the reader cannot even understand why the character is guarding this door or what they are trying to do right now should you check back and supply the already-existing necessary background.

## Determine Diagnostic Depth and Execution Authority Separately

| Can Be Applied in Ordinary De-slopping (可以在普通去味中落改) | Changes Requiring Plot-Design Authorization (需要剧情设计授权的改动) |
|---|---|
| Fixing verifiable slips in reference (指代), subject (主体), or chronology (时序) | Multiple interpretations are all valid, requiring choosing the true motive on the author's behalf |
| Restoring linkage of existing causality, conditions, and information sources | Adding new promises, evidence, character identities, abilities, events, or information sources |
| Expanding established confrontations, weighing of options, and feedback; adjusting level of detail within the same scene | Changing a character's decision, win/loss outcome, relationship trajectory, cost, or event sequence |
| Presenting known information within established revelation boundaries | Revealing the twist/truth early, moving foreshadowing, or changing the order in which reader and characters learn information |

Dialogue wording and necessary micro-links can serve established content; you cannot use a line of dialogue or an incidental action to add a threat, a promise, newly acquired evidence, or a change in character strategy. When switching POV (改换视角), narrate only the perceptions and cognition supported by that POV—do not add new experiences such as "discovered upon entering the door" (“进门时发现”) or "heard later" (“后来听说”) just to preserve information.

When the author has explicitly requested a rewrite or permitted plot adjustments, directly execute the authorized part while preserving all other established facts; do not ask for the same authorization again. When key grounds for a choice are still missing, propose minimal reviewable options. A proposed fix must specify which action, which condition, or which outcome it applies to, and explain which subsequent passages it will affect—do not just say "strengthen the conflict" (“加强冲突”). Put suggestions and speculation in the report; do not quietly turn them into main-text facts.

## Grouped Example: Outcomes Need a Process That Holds Up

The following is a synthetic example.

**Original text (原文)**: The shopkeeper accused the apprentice of altering the account book. The apprentice pulled out a copied ledger, everyone immediately believed him, and the shopkeeper admitted fault in public.
*(Original: 掌柜指认学徒改了账。学徒拿出一张抄本，众人立刻相信他，掌柜当众认错。)*

**Pinpointable diagnosis (可定位的诊断)**: Who kept the copied ledger, why it was trustworthy, and on what basis everyone had the ability and standing to verify it are all left unexplained. Simply pulling out a sheet of paper cannot prove what is written on the paper is true, and adding a few more sentences about bystanders being astonished cannot paper it over either.

**Ordinary de-slopping (普通去味)**: Clean out hollow modifiers and keep the established exoneration outcome; check earlier text for ledger-auditing conventions, independent master copies (底稿), or witness conditions. When current material lacks these, list the unfixed gap in the report and propose a minimal option plus the conditions that would need to be added, without treating the option as something that already happened.

**With prior setup and author-authorized dramatization (已有前情且作者授权演出)**: Previously, three clerks had each sealed their own master copies, and the shopkeeper allowed everyone to audit the accounts face-to-face.

> The apprentice placed his master copy on the counter and asked the other two to unseal those two sheets. The bottom line on all three copies showed the same figure, which did not match the new ledger in the shopkeeper's hands.
>
> Holding those sheets of paper, the shopkeeper admitted that this entry had not been altered by the apprentice.
>
> *(Original: 学徒把自己的底稿放在柜上，请另两人拆开封存的那两页。三份末行都是同一个数，和掌柜手中的新账对不上。 / 掌柜拿着那几页纸，承认这一笔不是学徒改的。)*

The weight of the evidence comes from the separately sealed master copies and face-to-face verification, without temporarily adding a secret witness. When the prior setup is already sufficient, write the verification directly—there is no need to manufacture another failure just to "add twists and turns" (“增加波折”).

**Directions that would break it (会改坏的方向)**: Suddenly adding an audio recording, a powerful relative, or a secret deal—making the outcome livelier while altering the story's basis; or changing the exoneration into a failure without authorization in order to "raise the cost" (“增加代价”) and decide consequences on the author's behalf.

## Post-Revision Read-Back

Put the revised passage back into the event chain before and after it, and verify the characters' order of knowing, basis for choice, outcome, and transition into the next scene. In the report, distinguish between gaps already fixed, parts still requiring design, and parts left inconclusive due to insufficient material. Smooth expression does not mean the story gap has disappeared.
