# Opening & Hooks Guide (`deep-story`)

> Pipeline: **Stage 1 (Golden 3 Chapters)** -> **Stage 2 (Opening Hook)** -> **Stage 3 (Ending Hook & Next-Chapter Handoff)**. Adjust pacing baselines to genre; never twist character choices just to hit rigid word counts.

---

## 1. Golden Three Chapters Pipeline (`Ch 1 -> Ch 2 -> Ch 3`)

**Core Formula**: `Hook = Immediate Dilemma (Ch1) + Escalating Verification/Cost (Ch2) + Long-Term Vector (Ch3)`
- Fast-paced default baseline: protagonist in active situation within ~300 chars, expectation established within ~1,000 chars (override when book style specifies slow-burn/atmospheric).

| Chapter | Required Checkpoints | Valid Chapter Ending |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ch 1** | Who, immediate situation/task, what makes the book worth reading (no terminology dump) | New discovery, choice, relationship shift, or concrete anticipation |
| **Ch 2** | How Ch 1 goal advances; how obstacles or understanding shift; concrete payoff lands | Partial payoff + concrete unfinished business |
| **Ch 3** | Stage progress delivered; long-term direction clear; stakeholders react by self-interest | Action pointing toward main plot or lingering aftertaste |

### Three Opening Anchors
1. **Character**: Show what they care about via an immediate trade-off, request for help, or sharp observation—not narrator praise.
2. **Entry Point**: Open in motion (conflict, opportunity, relationship, or flavorful routine) tied directly to the book's core appeal.
3. **Advantage (if any)**: Demonstrate one bounded use of the protagonist's edge/cheat; skip if writing ordinary-person realism.

> **Anchor (`❌ Background-First -> ✅ Task-Upfront`)**:
> - `❌` Reciting childhood biography and river-town history before mentioning the eviction deadline.
> - `✅` Open with the transfer list slammed on the counter (`month-end deadline + 7 customer watches + 1 square-ring vision fragment`), embedding backstory only where it explains the immediate stake.
> - **Ch 2 Handoff Rule**: Write concrete verification, claim withdrawal, quote acceptance, and payment as distinct actions—never summarize an entire confrontation as "he got slapped in the face."

### Opening Self-Check & Slow-Pacing Diagnosis

| Symptom / Check | Diagnostic Question | Fix Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main plot unclear** | Why is the protagonist staying here? | State immediate task + wagered stakes in scene 1 |
| **Dialogue spins** | Did the situation change after the talk? | Cut pleasantries; keep only probing, clashes, and shifts |
| **Worldbuilding drowns action** | Is all this lore needed right now? | Keep only rules required for the immediate conflict |
| **Flashbacks stall** | Does this past event alter present judgment? | Keep the single decisive stroke; compress the rest |
| **All-talk wins** | Did the decision-maker or payer actually act? | Show concrete action, concession, or delivered payment |
| **Blurb mismatch** | Do title, blurb, and Ch 1–3 deliver the same flavor? | Align tone and promise; never force humiliation/crowd-gasp templates |

## 2. Chapter-Opening Hooks (7 Types)

Pick based on what the previous chapter left open: **Impact** (6, 3), **Curiosity** (1, 2, 4), or **Contrast** (5, 7).

| # | Type | Formula | Concise Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Suspense Dialogue** | Open mid-exchange loaded with subtext | "Did you see him?" "Saw him." "Did he recognize you?" |
| 2 | **Flash-Forward Fragment** | Show outcome fragment, then rewind to normal flow | Sirens stopped downstairs at 3 a.m. Twelve hours earlier, she was still arguing over a utility bill. |
| 3 | **Countdown Opening** | Establish time pressure in sentence 1 | Forty hours left until the demolition crew entered the village. |
| 4 | **Mysterious Monologue** | Strange first-person observation (1st-POV only) | He knocked nine times—I counted—yet I only opened the door once. |
| 5 | **Contrast Scene** | Juxtapose two starkly opposite scenes | Front courtyard: birthday banquet gongs. Back well: someone washing a muddy shovel. |
| 6 | **Unfinished Action** | Cut off an action mid-motion | Just as he tore the envelope corner, someone outside called his childhood nickname. |
| 7 | **Imagery Foreshadowing** | Environmental cue hinting incoming trouble | A row of paper lanterns drifted downriver, all unlit. She closed the window. |

## 3. Chapter-Ending Hooks (13 Types + 10 Practical Templates)

**Suspense Formula**: `Surprise = bomb explodes (readers move on); Suspense = ticking timer visible (readers turn page)`.

| # | Type | Formula | Concise Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Sudden Revelation** | Drop fact rewriting the situation | The warehouse signer on camera was wearing her old raincoat. |
| 2 | **Urgent Crisis** | Unavoidable threat for next chapter | River water passed the third step, and the boat still wasn't fixed. |
| 3 | **Unfinished Action** | Action interrupted by new variable | Before her fingertip touched the doorbell, it rang first. |
| 4 | **Identity Reversal** | True identity breaks reader assumption | The new bookkeeper's seal belonged to the household ruined three years ago. |
| 5 | **Dilemma Choice** | Force pick between two costly options | Hand over the ledger and Master lives; refuse and every shop on this street dies. |
| 6 | **Mysterious Object** | Reveal crucial item with unclear meaning | Under the lunchbox lay red cloth wrapping a baby tooth, her birthday stitched on the corner. |
| 7 | **Countdown** | Show time visibly running out | Ten minutes before the exam bell; his admission ticket was still in the taxi. |
| 8 | **Promise / Threat** | Openly declare imminent action | "Before daybreak, I'm selling this shop—along with you." |
| 9 | **Bizarre Disappearance** | Logically impossible vanishing | One line of footprints led into the woodshed; inside, no one. |
| 10 | **Hidden Meaning** | Flaw hidden inside casual remark | "Give my regards to that osmanthus downstairs." The tree was planted only yesterday. |
| 11 | **Imagery Hook** | Recurring motif suddenly shifts | The window lit until ten every night went dark at eight today. |
| 12 | **Echo Hook** | Repeat opening line with altered key detail | Opening: "walked with eyes closed." Ending: "opened his eyes and stopped at the intersection." |
| 13 | **Blank-Space Hook** | Show reaction only, withhold cause | After hanging up, she turned off the stove and walked out without locking the door. |

**10 Quick Scene Templates**: `System-Activation` (cheat online at rock bottom) · `Suspense-Foresight` (coldly watch rival gloat) · `Cut-Off` (small act triggers sharp question) · `Choice` (two tempting/costly options) · `Crisis-Escalation` (crash + new threat) · `Secret-Graze` (bystander remark grazes hidden identity) · `Emotional-Turn` (abrupt warmth/coldness shift) · `Information-Gap` (self-doubt + omen) · `Dialogue-Suspense` (second half of request shocks room) · `Time-Jump` (time marker + unexpected outcome).

### Selection by Chapter Responsibility & AI-Ending Fixes

| Chapter Job | Best Landing Points | Next Chapter Must Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anomaly Discovered** | Object, info shift | Verify, rule out, or act on it |
| **Pressure Incoming** | Countdown, crisis | Respond directly without switching topics |
| **Victory Completed** | New duty, relationship, aftertaste | Acknowledge earned payoff |
| **Relationship Shift** | Request, choice, emotion | Let other party respond |
| **Low-Pressure Daily** | Small goal, quiet emotion | Advance naturally without fake secrets |
| **Book Finale** | Fulfillment + echo | Close without opening new suspense |

> **Ending Repair Formula (`❌ Vague Teaser -> ✅ Concrete Lead`)**:
> - `❌` *"She closed the ledger, finally understanding his intentions. Little did she know a massive vortex awaited."* (Violates limited POV + empty "vortex" filler.)
> - `✅` Pick **one** concrete stop: either `Object` (ferry ticket dated next month in missing father's name) OR `Action` (circling "Old East-Bank Pier" to ask tomorrow morning)—never cram both, and stop on the exact sentence where suspense peaks.
> - **Next-Chapter Pickup**: Never dodge the hook with *"more secrets ahead"*; immediately verify the concrete lead (ticket serial number / phone log) to move the thread forward by one solid step.
