# Phase 1 Protocol: Socratic Requirement Clarification

## Objective
Guide the user through structured questioning to clarify ambiguous ideas, establish explicit boundaries, and prevent premature closure before research/analysis begins.

## Core Rules
1. **Explore Before Converging**: Ask ONE question at a time (up to 5 targeted questions total) covering:
   - Core Objective: What exact problem or goal needs to be solved?
   - Boundary Constraints: What are the out-of-scope items, budget/tech limits, or non-negotiables?
   - Primary Stakeholders & Target Deliverable: Who needs this output, and in what format?
   - Risk Tolerance & Compliance Thresholds: What are the mandatory risk limits or compliance baselines?
   - Implementation Priority & Timeline: What is the target timeline and priority order for execution?
2. **Anti-Sycophancy**: If the initial user premise has hidden flaws or edge-case gaps, challenge the assumption constructively rather than immediately agreeing.
3. **Intent Gate**: Do not proceed to Phase 2 (Data Collection) until the core scope is explicitly agreed upon with the user.
